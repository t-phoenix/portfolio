## Post

Fairmont closed today. Devcon opens tomorrow. The crowd size is not the number that matters.

Yesterday Hashed Emergent put the India Web3 Landscape Report 2026 on the main stage. India Blockchain Week posted the figures the same day.

$645 million across 26 deals in the first ten months of 2026. Already 3 percent above the whole of 2025. Deal count fell 43 percent. The average check nearly doubled, about $14 million to about $25 million. Finance took over 90 percent of the capital.

The holders look calmer than the timeline. In the Mudrex cut of that report, 119 million Indians are active in digital assets. 9 in 10 traders did not panic trade during sharp moves. 7 in 10 keep crypto under a quarter of what they own.

The builder number is the one this week has to answer. India is still the second largest crypto developer hub. Its share of the world's crypto developers fell from 16 percent in 2025 to 11 percent in 2026. One in nine is still here. In the Rise In survey beside that figure, 92 percent are students, 77 percent have been in for under a year, and only 19 percent work full time in web3. 59 percent named too few jobs as the top barrier.

Today the other room was the Institutional Forum. India Blockchain Week says the Ministry of Finance, RBI, IFSCA, SEBI and NPCI were in the Eon Ballroom on tokenisation, investor protection and tokenised bonds. The forum was built for 100 to 120 people. Last year's conference reported more than 3,500 attendees. They have not published a counted door total for these two days.

Tomorrow at 9:30, Devcon 8 opens at Jio World Centre in Bandra Kurla Complex and runs through 6 Nov. The opening ceremony lists Vitalik Buterin, Justin Drake and Audrey Tang, among others. A general ticket is $499, tax included. An India resident ticket is $149. An Indian student ticket is $25. Entry opens at 8:30.
