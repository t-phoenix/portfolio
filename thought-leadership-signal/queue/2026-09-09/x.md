## Post (thread)

### 1
Soft edges fail first.

AI does not need to break the cryptography to shake the system.

### 2
Vitalik Buterin just pushed back on a claim that AI could drive a 50% Bitcoin crash inside two years.

Coverage landed across CryptoSlate, Yahoo Finance, and TheStreet. The fight is not vibes. It is where the risk actually sits.

### 3
His frame is useful for builders.

He puts low odds on AI breaking hashing or proof of work.
He is optimistic on long term cybersecurity.
He says the primary problem is the transition.

And his holdings already bet about 90% of his net worth on that view.

### 4
The scary part is already visible at the edges.

In August, Bitcoin swap provider Boltz paused after months of automated, AI assisted probing moved faster than a small team could patch. Non custodial design protected users. The service still could not keep running safely.

Clients. Mining pools. Wallets. Bridges. Exchanges. Ops tempo.

That is the soft surface area.

### 5
Defenders are racing too.

Anthropic pointed Mythos class capability at defense through Project Glasswing, with up to $100M in usage credits.
More than 100 organizations, including Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, Visa, and Mastercard, signed an open letter warning that AI cyberattacks will get worse in months, not years.

### 6
So the Web3 x AI lesson is not "AI ends Bitcoin."

It is this: systems with transferable value and public surfaces will feel the transition first.

Build for the soft edges, or the transition will choose for you.

If AI probing hit your stack tomorrow, what fails first: code, keys, or human ops?
