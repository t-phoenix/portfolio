## Post

### 1
Solana has two words for done. Confirmed and finalized. The next upgrade deletes the gap between them.

### 2
Today full finality takes about 12.8 seconds. Alpenglow targets 100 to 150 milliseconds. It has been live on testnet since late September. Mainnet has no confirmed date, and a tentative September 28 start already passed.

### 3
The quieter change is where the votes go. About 7 in 10 Solana transactions today are validators voting on blocks. Alpenglow moves those votes off the ledger into direct messages between validators.

### 4
Here is why I care. Every wait before finality is a loan. An exchange, a bridge, or a merchant either waits or carries the risk for you and charges for it. Shrink the wait and that job shrinks with it.

### 5
It is not free. The old design needed two thirds of stake honest and online. The new one tolerates 20% hostile plus 20% offline. Speed was bought with a narrower margin. That is a trade, not a bug.
