# Brief — 2026-10-08

## Pillar
macro-finance, through settlement time. Web3 only. Not a sequel to the 10-07 enforcement thesis.

## Receipt
Solana Alpenglow is live on testnet since late September. It replaces TowerBFT with Votor. Confirmed and finalized become one state. Votes move off the ledger. Mainnet has no confirmed date.

## Angle
Finality is not a speed stat. It is the moment risk changes hands. Every wait before it is a loan someone prices.

## Hook
Solana has two words for done. The next upgrade deletes the gap between them.

## Numbers (caption only)
- Finality about 12.8 s today, target 100 to 150 ms
- Vote transactions average 71.5% of all transactions
- Old model needs two thirds of stake honest and online. New model tolerates 20% adversarial plus 20% offline
- Tentative September 28 start passed without activation

## Emotional bridge
Anyone who has waited on a deposit, a bridge, or a payout knows the feeling. Someone is holding your risk and you are paying for it.

## Insight
Find the wait. Find who carries it. That is who a faster chain puts out of work.

## Comment invite
Which part of your product only exists because settlement is slow?

## Why we are early
Headlines sell the speed. The collapse of two finality states, the vote share of the ledger, and the narrower fault margin are in validator docs.
