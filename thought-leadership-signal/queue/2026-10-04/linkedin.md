## Post

A fast yes is cheap. A yes that moves money is not.

Jev is genuinely good at the cheap job. You bound the question. It answers quickly. It attaches a confidence number your code can read. I want that in places where a wrong guess is annoying and reversible. I do not want it holding keys.

Look at the bots that sound serious rather than viral. They do not ask the model what to buy and then obey. They let it refuse. A rule, a size limit, or a person still has to approve before value moves. The model gets a vote. The wallet stays with something that can be fired, capped, or audited.

I recognized the shape from credit, not from demo day.

A signal is not a facility. In private credit, someone owns the loss. There is a balance sheet. There is a covenant. There is a human who can stop the draw. A faster signal does not delete that layer. It just arrives earlier, which makes the gate more important, not less.

Three checks, before any model touches capital.

Is the question closed, or did you secretly ask it to invent a plan.

Can something else veto, without negotiating with the model.

Who eats a wrong yes. If the answer is "the user, later, confused," you do not have a system. You have a story.

When you put a model in the loop, did you give it a vote, or the wallet?
