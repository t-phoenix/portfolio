# Topic selection (Signal)

Pillars in [`TOPICS.yml`](TOPICS.yml) are a menu, not a mandate to mash Web3 and AI every day.

## Rotate freely

| Mode | When | Examples |
|------|------|----------|
| **Web3 only** | DeFi, contracts, settlement, protocol eng, macro money | AMM design, exploit postmortem, stablecoin rails |
| **AI only** | LLMs, agents, MCP, skills, tooling, evals | MCP auth gaps, agent harnesses, model releases |
| **Intersection** | When the receipt is genuinely both | x402 agent payments, onchain agents |
| **Builder ops** | Shipping truth without forcing a thesis | failure modes, security, culture |

Do **not** force a Web3 x AI bridge when the strongest receipt is one sided.

## Anti-repeat

- Never reuse yesterday’s thesis with a new label (permission → identity was too close)
- Prefer a **new mechanism** or **new receipt**, not a sequel slide of the same week arc
- Scan last 3 packs’ hooks before locking today’s angle

## Technical depth

- Technical is welcome. Tasteful **code snippets** on creatives are allowed when they are the receipt (one smoking gun line > a wall of code)
- Numbers and long code stay in copy when the slide would get busy
- Still: no hyphens/dashes in outward posts; no product CTAs
