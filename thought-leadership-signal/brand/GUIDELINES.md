## Creative quality bar (non negotiable)

Creatives must look like work from a top branding and creative agency.

**Pass the agency test**
- Would this sit next to a Stripe, Linear, or Bloomberg Edit graphic without looking amateur?
- Typography, spacing, and hierarchy feel intentional, not templated
- Real world texture: photography, marks, charts, receipts from reality
- Zero generic AI illustration (glow brains, neon grids, fake 3D coins, invented faces)

**Craft standards**
- Consistent folio, margins, and type system within a pack
- **Every carousel slide is a hero** — same stop power as slide 01; no supporting filler frames
- One dominant idea per frame, big type, safe inset, no cut text
- Phone legible at arm length
- Alternating rhythm across days; never the same layout template forever

## Verbal brand

- Clear, analytical, story led
- Pragmatic utility over hype
- Systems through history and human cost of bad coordination
- No hyphens or dashes in outward posts
- Prefer mechanisms and mental models over slogans

## Content hard rules

**Do**
- Lead with a scroll stop creative built from real assets
- Ground posts in news, social discourse, or lived stories (cited in research)
- Use real numbers and charts when the data exists
- Teach something reusable; invite substantive comments

**Do not**
- Farm engagement with empty bait
- Invent statistics or fake dashboards
- Use AI imaginary scenes as the hero visual
- Shill tokens, products, or follow CTAs

## Signature themes

1. Coordination at scale beats lone genius products
2. Web3 x AI as resilient infrastructure, not novelty
3. Culture is part of the protocol stack
4. Markets and money as designed systems, not magic
