# Engagement Doctrine

Goal: become a trusted thought leader over years, not a viral farmer for a week.

## Farming vs value

| Farming (banned) | Long term value (required) |
|------------------|----------------------------|
| "Agree?" with no substance | A reusable mental model |
| Fake urgency / outrage bait | Real stakes from a real story |
| Vague hot takes | Mechanism, number, or checklist |
| Stolen screenshots without insight | Receipt + interpretation |
| Engagement pods energy | Comments that teach you something back |

## What makes people stay

1. **Recognition** — "that happened to me"
2. **Clarity** — a frame they can reuse tomorrow
3. **Receipts** — news, posts, numbers they can verify
4. **Respect** — you do not insult their intelligence
5. **Series memory** — ideas that compound across weeks

## Every pack must include

At least **two** of:

- A **real world hook** (news headline, social thread, on chain event, personal builder story)
- A **number or comparison** grounded in cited research (volume, upvotes, dates, fees, TVL, users). Put detailed numbers in **post text**, not duplicated on the creative.
- An **emotional bridge** tied to a human situation (trust break, wasted night, coordination failure)
- A **durable insight** (mental model, checklist, historical parallel)

Creatives carry the scroll stop and the frame. Captions carry the receipts and math.

## Comment design (not bait)

Ask questions only a person with experience can answer well.

Good: "Where did an agent last act against your interest?"  
Good: "Which layer failed first for you: intelligence, rails, or culture?"  
Bad: "Thoughts?"  
Bad: "Like if you agree"

## Compounding system

- Recycle evergreen models across months with new receipts
- Reference prior Signal posts lightly when a model returns (builds a body of work)
- Prefer teaching depth over daily novelty theater
- Track in research what topics earned substantive comments; lean into those mechanisms

## Success signals (directional)

- Saves and bookmarks
- Comments that add cases or push back with evidence
- DMs that ask for the model, not for alpha
- People quoting the frame weeks later
