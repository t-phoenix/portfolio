# Engagement Doctrine

Goal: become a trusted thought leader over years, not a viral farmer for a week.
Reach without trust is spam. Trust without reach is a private diary. Signal needs both.

**Virality research (required read for drafters):** [`research/virality/`](../research/virality/) — `playbook.md`, `x.yml`, `linkedin.yml`.

## Farming vs value

| Farming (banned) | Long term value (required) |
|------------------|----------------------------|
| "Agree?" with no substance | A reusable mental model |
| Fake urgency / outrage bait | Real stakes from a real story |
| Vague hot takes | Mechanism, number, or checklist |
| Stolen screenshots without insight | Receipt + interpretation |
| Engagement pods / spam tagging | Comments that teach you something back |
| "Comment YES" emoji chains | A fork people can defend with a case |

## What makes people stay

1. **Recognition** — "that happened to me"
2. **Clarity** — a frame they can reuse tomorrow
3. **Receipts** — news, posts, numbers they can verify
4. **Respect** — you do not insult their intelligence
5. **Series memory** — ideas that compound across weeks

## What makes people comment (required)

Every pack needs a **comment magnet**: a fork or lived ask answerable in one sentence.

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Binary fork | "Mainnet default or Base default. One reason." |
| Layer pick | "What failed first: cost, custody, or liquidity?" |
| Lived slot | "Where does your product still pretend L1 is the money path?" |
| Disagree invite | "Where is this wrong for institutions?" |

Ban: "Thoughts?" · "Agree?" · "Like if" · empty "What do you think?"

Author ops: seed first reply (sources on X), answer the first 5 comments same day. Early replies are distribution fuel on both platforms.

## Every pack must include

At least **two** of:

- A **real world hook** (news headline, social thread, on chain event, personal builder story)
- A **number or comparison** grounded in cited research (volume, upvotes, dates, fees, TVL, users). Put detailed numbers in **post text**, not duplicated on the creative (except `data_frame` / `hero_billboard` where the contrast IS the art).
- An **emotional bridge** tied to a human situation (trust break, wasted night, coordination failure)
- A **durable insight** (mental model, checklist, historical parallel)

Plus always:

- Comment magnet (above)
- ≤ 3 relevant tags / mentions tied to the receipt
- Source links (LinkedIn in body after insight; X in first reply by default)

Creatives carry the scroll stop and the frame. Captions carry the receipts and math.

## Tags and links

- Tag only entities **named in the receipt** (protocols, issuers, data vendors, publishers).
- Max 3 on X, max 3 on LinkedIn. No friend tagging for reach.
- Name the source in plain text so the click is intentional.

## Format for growth (2026-10+)

Prefer **one hero creative** (`hero_billboard`, `meme_real`, `receipt_collage`, `data_frame`) while the account is under ~2k X followers. Carousels teach well but have underperformed for reach here ([`GROWTH_PLAN.md`](../GROWTH_PLAN.md)).

## Compounding system

- Recycle evergreen models across months with new receipts
- Reference prior Signal posts lightly when a model returns (builds a body of work)
- Prefer teaching depth over daily novelty theater
- Track in research what topics earned substantive comments; lean into those mechanisms
- Run daily replies into watchlist threads; that is still the main growth lever

## Success signals (directional)

- Replies / comments with cases or pushback
- Saves and bookmarks
- Copy link / reshare
- DMs that ask for the model, not for alpha
- People quoting the frame weeks later
- Views rising above the ~30 median Signal baseline
