## Post

Base cleared more stablecoin volume than Ethereum mainnet.

Over 90 days, 15 July to 12 October 2026, Token Terminal counted every stablecoin. Base $8.5T. Mainnet $7.1T.

The useful question is where USDC on Base actually went. Talos and Coin Metrics looked at that cut for 2026. Two apps did most of it.

Aerodrome is the exchange. You trade one token for another. Liquidity sits in pools. It carried 69% of USDC volume on Base.

Morpho is the lending market. Suppliers deposit assets. Borrowers draw them out. It carried 23%.

About 8% went through other apps. That is a footnote, not the story.

Do not multiply 69% by $8.5T. One number is USDC on Base. The other is every stablecoin, over 90 days.

If you build on Base, the map is short. An exchange. A lending market. Then everything else.

Which one do you actually use?
