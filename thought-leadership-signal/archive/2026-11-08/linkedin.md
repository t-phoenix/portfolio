## Post

I don't collect swag.

Then I spent 7 days in Mumbai. @India Blockchain Week, @ETHGlobal Mumbai and Pragma, then Devcon by the @Ethereum Foundation. I came home with a table full of it, and honestly it surprised me.

So here is the honest review.

The badges. Most event badges are a laminated card on a strap. For a week it felt like a leash around my neck. ETHGlobal was the exception: thick, matte, clean, actually designed. This industry has fixed memes, merch, fonts and onboarding. Nobody has fixed the badge. Make it NFC, make it a wearable POAP, make it something people want to keep.

The best swag was 3D printed. Two @ENS Labs fidget spinners. Small, useless, perfect. Those stay on the desk. Most of the stickers go in a drawer.

Only two shirts made it home: @Network School and Ethereum. I almost never take event tees. A logo on your chest is an endorsement, and I only wear the ones I would defend in a room full of skeptics. Free is not a reason.

(The last slide is a paper doll parody. Vitalik and Kartik did not pose for it.)

What you keep from a week like this says more than what you were handed.

If you were in Mumbai: what should the 2027 event badge actually be? Drop it below. Best idea gets a sticker off this table, and tag the one friend who still has their lanyard on.
