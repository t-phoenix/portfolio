# Ownback launch · brief (owner request, 2026-11-19)

**Goal:** get followers to try Ownback (app.ownback.tech, free for the first 1,000), then come back and leave a reaction and a comment with what they'd own.

**Thesis (the controversy):** Cashback, points and loyalty cards are built to keep you a customer and never make you an owner. Apple took $8,250 from Abhinil in ten years. He owns zero Apple shares.

**Why it's blunt and still honest:**
- The numbers come from the intro film and its narration script: $8,250 spent, about $38,000 if invested, and $412 set aside at 5% that would be worth about $1,900. Say "would be", never "you will make".
- The Bumped story is true: it raised $30M and shut down in Dec 2022 after merchants stopped paying. Source: business/funding-roadmap.md.
- "No token. No brand budget. Your own money, your signature." This is the real difference from SP3ND, Crumbs and Receipt. Ownback is not the only "stock back" app, so never claim it is the first.

**The engagement mechanic (try → screenshot → comment):**
1. Zero-friction try: on ownback.tech, "Scratch to see what you'd own" works without signing up and takes about 10 seconds.
2. Real try: app.ownback.tech, with Google sign-in.
3. Ask: "Reply with your YOU'D OWN screenshot. I'll reply to every one with what ten years of that habit would be worth."
4. Reaction: "Like this if you've bought more iPhones than you own Apple shares." It's an easy yes for almost everyone.

**Hard limits to respect:**
- xStocks are not for US persons, and the app geo-blocks the US. Say so in the link reply, so US followers play with the scratch cards instead of hitting a wall.
- Add "Not financial advice" wherever shares are mentioned.
- On X: one cashtag per post, at most 3 @tags per tweet, links only in the first reply.

**Brand direction for any creative:** B (Mumbai Festival) or C (Sticker Bomb Zine). The last pack was A. Use the real Ownback assets in Ownback/business/socials and the website-stills.
