# Funds and grants for distribution · who to tag and what to say

Grounded in Ownback/business/funding-roadmap.md (researched 10 Nov 2026). Confirm each program's terms on its own page before you apply.

**The ask, in one line:** "Ownback is live on mainnet. I need $15k to $50k to get the first 1,000 owners. The fees are capped at 5% and we take nothing from them."

**Fill in before sending:** [users], [receipts scanned], [buys signed], [launch post link]. Use real numbers only. If you have no users yet, say "launched {date}" and send it 7 days after the launch post, once you have some.

## Public post (X, day 2 or 3, after the launch thread has replies)

Tag at most 3 accounts. Tag the ones you actually built on, so it reads as proof, not begging.

```
Every ecosystem says it wants consumer apps that bring normal people onchain.

Here's one. Live on mainnet. Built in 36 hours. [users] people own a piece of what they bought in week one.

Now the blunt part: I'm raising grants for distribution, not another hackathon prize.

@SuperteamIN @arc @0G_labs, who's in?
```

Put the launch post as a quote, and the deck PDF link in the first reply.

**Why these three:** they're all low copy risk (the roadmap rates them low), they're tied to Ownback's actual stack, and they're India-friendly. Keep Kraken, Bitget and Robinhood out of public tags. Kraken and Bitget already power or run stock-back apps, and Crumbs is already Robinhood's horse.

## Private outreach (where the money actually comes from)

Apply at https://in.superteam.fun/instagrants/solana-foundation-coindcx-india-grants

```
Ownback turns everyday spending into ownership. 5% of any receipt goes into USDC jars, and the user signs each buy of tokenized shares of the company, its suppliers and the raw materials. It's live on mainnet, and the buys settle on Solana via Jupiter.

Ask: $15k for distribution in India. Campus ambassadors through Superteam chapters, fee-free first buys for 1,000 users, and receipt-to-ownership content.
Milestones: 300 users in 30 days → 1,000 users, with ≥300 making a real buy, in 90 days. Weekly public dashboard.
Proof: [launch post link] · demo video · mainnet vault on Arc.
```

### 2. Superteam Moonshot Fund · $20k
Apply at https://in.superteam.fun/instagrants/moonshots. Reuse the text above and lead with their own words: "a consumer app that brings thousands of new users onchain".

Apply at https://www.circle.com/grant, and join the Builders Fund waitlist at https://www.arc.io/builders-fund

```
Subject: Ownback: consumer savings on Arc, live on mainnet

It's a consumer reason to hold USDC every day, from people who have never touched crypto. Built at ETHGlobal Mumbai (Arc track), and the vault is live: 0x2731…B864.

Ask: a milestone grant to take it from [users] to 1,000 USDC savers. 15 minutes to show you the demo?
```
