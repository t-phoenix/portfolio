# Slides — 2026-10-31

One studio, five spatial ideas. Teal is the new job. Amber is value moving. Dark metal is the lock. Paper is an ordinary signature.

1. The door. A shut vault, a desk, papers leaving the desk. They never took the key.
2. The pages. A fan of pages, one teal page seated in the wallet. Fifty pages. One fit.
3. The permission. A glass wallet, coins still inside, a sheet approaching the face. That signature sent nothing.
4. The split. One wallet keeps the sheet and spills coins into a tray. The other has already let a single paper go. One wallet kept the job.
5. The cleanup. Sheet torn off, same wheel on the old box, coins in a new box. Same lock. New address.
