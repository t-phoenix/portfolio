## Post

Base just cleared more stablecoin volume than Ethereum mainnet.

Read that again.

Over 90 days (15 Jul to 12 Oct 2026), Token Terminal data cited by Ethereum Institutional and covered by CryptoBriefing shows:

→ Base: $8.5T in stablecoin transfer volume  
→ Ethereum mainnet: $7.1T  
→ Optimism: $0.8T  
→ Other Ethereum L2s: $0.35T  

Together, Ethereum and its L2s settled $16.75T. That is about 77% of the $21.55T moved across all networks in the window.

The headline will be "Ethereum still dominates dollars." That is true at the family level.

The product map is sharper.

The dollar did not leave Ethereum. It left the expensive floor.

Most of that flow is institutional transfer and DeFi (flash loans, liquidity), not someone buying coffee. And a trillion dollars moving on Base pays Base first. Settlement share and fee capture are different jobs.

I have watched teams argue brand ("we are an L1 shop") while the settlement file quietly routes to the cheap rail. Operators already voted with gas.

Brand loyalty is not a settlement rail. Find where the dollars clear. Build there.

If you ship a money product tomorrow, do you default to mainnet or Base first?

Reply with one reason. Cost, custody, liquidity, or something I am missing.
