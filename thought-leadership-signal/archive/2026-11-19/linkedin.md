## Post

Everyone who bought gold at the record high is now a long term investor.

Gold touched about $5,600 in January. Today it is near $3,730, down 33%. Bitcoin peaked near $126,000 in October 2025. Today it is near $63,700, down 50%.

The feed says run. I went back through 80 years of money crises instead. 1944, 1971, 2008, 2020, and now. The same thing happens every time, and it is not what the headlines tell you.

1. The crash
October US inflation came in hot at 0.4% against 0.3% expected. The dollar hit a 19 month high. Markets started pricing a Fed hike, not a cut. US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw about $1.9 billion leave in seven straight trading days. Funds sold whatever they could sell.

2. The pattern
In a panic, even gold gets sold first. In 2008 it fell 29% before running 170% to $1,921 by 2011. In March 2020 it fell 15% before setting a new record that August. Margin calls do not care what a safe haven is. The dash for cash always comes before the money printer.

3. The buyer
The rich do not predict crashes. They wait for someone else to panic. In 2008 Warren Buffett lent Goldman Sachs $5 billion at a 10% dividend. In 2020 the big buyers loaded up the moment the Fed promised unlimited support, then sold hyped listings to retail in 2021. Today the quiet buyer is central banks: more than 1,000 tonnes of gold a year since 2022, while Western ETFs sell.

4. Your move
You cannot out trade them. You can out wait them, because nobody is asking you for quarterly results.
Never be a forced seller: six months of cash, zero leverage.
Buy in slices, not bottoms.
Rebalance to a number you wrote down before the news hit.

One opinion that will annoy my own industry: Bitcoin is not digital gold. Not yet. It fell 63% in March 2020, 77% in 2022, and 50% now, more than stocks every single time. I still build in web3 and I still think fixed supply matters. But today it trades like leveraged tech, so size it like leveraged tech.

None of this is financial advice. It is just the pattern.
