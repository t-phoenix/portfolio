## Post

Soft edges fail first.

AI does not need to break the cryptography to shake the system.

Over the last few days, Vitalik Buterin pushed back on a high profile claim that AI could help drive a 50% Bitcoin crash within two years. The debate traveled beyond crypto native feeds. CryptoSlate covered it in depth. Yahoo Finance and TheStreet amplified it.

The useful part is not the celebrity argument. It is the mental model.

Buterin puts low probability on AI producing a fundamental break in hashing or proof of work. He is optimistic about cybersecurity in the long run. He says the primary problem is getting through the transition. His existing holdings already put roughly 90% of his net worth on that side of the bet.

That matches what builders feel on the ground.

In August, Bitcoin swap provider Boltz indefinitely suspended service after months of automated, AI assisted probing. Exploits were contained. Customer funds were protected by a non custodial design. The team still concluded it could not safely keep the service running. Automation found and stressed weaknesses faster than humans could investigate, patch, and ship.

That is the soft edge pattern: clients, mining pools, wallets, bridges, exchanges, and ops tempo. Not the base consensus ritual.

Defenders know it. Anthropic restricted public access to Claude Mythos style offensive capability and steered it toward defense through Project Glasswing, committing up to $100 million in usage credits. More than 100 organizations, including Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, Visa, and Mastercard, signed an open letter warning that AI enabled cyberattacks will become substantially more widespread within months.

Web3 x AI is not a slogan here. It is a race condition.

Intelligence accelerates both sides.
Rails and culture decide who absorbs the blast radius during the lag.

Build for the soft edges, or the transition will choose for you.

If AI probing hit your stack tomorrow, what fails first: code, keys, or human ops?
