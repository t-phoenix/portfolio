### 2
Yesterday the world’s largest asset manager published a paper on the machine native economy.

Their near term bet is simple.
Autonomous agents will buy compute and data.
Stablecoins are the money that can price tiny always on payments better than cards or ACH.

### 3
The paper also floats a longer horizon: tokenized claims on computing capacity as a future market.

That is the thesis layer.
It is coherent.
It is also still mostly a forecast.

### 4
Decrypt carried a useful reality check from TRM Labs.

They looked at about $52.7M in x402 settlements this year.
The share that looked like genuine AI agents sat between 0.6% and 7.5%.
Most of the rest looked more like ordinary automation.

BlackRock’s own framing puts stablecoin market cap above $300B as of September 2026, with more than $11T in adjusted volume in 2025.
The money rail is real.
The agent payer identity is still thin.

### 5
I keep three checks before I believe agent demand:

1. Unit of account (stable price for machine quotes)
2. Always on rails (settlement that does not wait for banking hours)
3. Identity of the payer (agent vs script vs human API key)

Thesis without receipt is marketing.
Receipt without thesis is noise.
