## Report figures posted by @IBWofficial on 1 Nov

Funding: $645M across 26 deals in the first 10 months of 2026. Already 3% above full year 2025. Deal count fell 43%. Average round nearly doubled, about $25M from about $14M. Finance took over 90% of capital.

Users, with Mudrex: 119M+ Indians active in digital assets. 60% aged 25 to 44. Women 16%. 40% outside metros. 9 in 10 traders did not panic trade during sharp moves. 70%+ of SIP investors kept buying through 2025 corrections. 7 in 10 allocate under 25% of wealth to crypto.

Builders, with Rise In: 1 in 9 crypto developers worldwide is in India. Still the number 2 hub. Share fell from 16% in 2025 to 11% in 2026. Cities: Bengaluru 17%, Delhi NCR 15%, Mumbai 9%. Survey: 96% aged 18 to 25, 92% students, 77% under a year, Solidity 47%, Rust 43%, only 19% full time in web3, 59% name limited jobs as the top barrier, 62% have been paid in crypto. Chains in that survey: Ethereum 32%, Solana 32%.

A March 2026 press release of an earlier cut (Business Standard, data as of March) had different totals, including a 15.2% developer share and $626M for a prior window. The post uses the 1 Nov stage thread only, and says so.
