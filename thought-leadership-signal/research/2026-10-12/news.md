# Research — 2026-10-12

## Angle
Continuous governance as infrastructure. Election cadence lag. India as lived scale case. Not a party hit piece.

## Receipts

### Edelman Trust Barometer 2026 (India)
- India Trust Index remains relatively high (about 75 on the multi market index; government trust about 80% in the global report tables).
- India specific signal: trust is rising yet becoming more insular. Nearly half prefer people who think like them, read the same media, or share values.
- Trust inequality: about 16 point gap between higher and lower income groups in India.
- Global: government has a large expectation vs performance gap on bridging divides (about 42 points).
- Sources:
  - https://www.edelman.com/in/trust/2026/trust-barometer

### Stanford GSB working paper (Hall & Miyazaki, Oct 2024)
- Liquid democracy studied in high stakes online platforms.
- Over 250,000 voters, 1,700 proposals, 18 Ethereum based DAOs.
- About 17% of voting tokens delegated on average. Power clumps on popular delegates.
- Delegation hubs raise delegation and voting rates.
- Participation among voters and delegates remains a hard unsolved challenge.
- https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/working-papers/what-happens-when-anyone-can-be-your-representative-studying-use

### vTaiwan / Pol.is
- Civic led online offline process. Four stages: proposal, opinion, reflection, legislation.
- Used to help craft about 26 pieces of digital economy related legislation with public consensus tools.
- Pol.is maps agreement and disagreement so organizers can find overlapping majorities.
- https://congress.crowd.law/case-vtaiwan.html
- https://participedia.net/method/vtaiwan

### Estonia X-Road
- Secure inter organizational data exchange with authentication, encryption, access control, and audit logs.
- Legal mandate for public bodies to exchange via X-Road. Private sector can join.
- Trust as interoperability rules, not a once in five years ceremony.
- https://cyber.ee/uploads/T_4_1_X_Road_complete_solution_for_inter_organizational_information_exchange_bf61cb2da1.pdf

## India framing (honest)
Do not claim Edelman shows collapsing trust in Indian government. It does not.
Use Oct 10 as a process receipt (sealed square, security answer before deliberation), not as a party endorsement.
Use Edelman for: high aggregate trust + growing insularity + income trust gap.
Mechanism claim: when media, courts, and election machinery are experienced as aligned with power rather than checking it, people stop auditing process and start picking tribes.
Tech thesis: continuous feedback, public audit trails, and costly rule breaking beat a single ballot every four or five years.
